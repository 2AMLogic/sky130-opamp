## Interpretation

- **The unity-buffer swing shortfall at SS / -40 C (0.948 Vpp, 58.5 % of 1.62 V) is set by the input
  stage's common-mode range in the follower connection, not by the output stage.** High edge 1.203 V:
  the input pair runs out of drain headroom as its common mode rises (1.203 V is 34 mV above this
  point's 40 dB ICMR high edge, 1.169 V; the follower's 0.8-slope criterion is looser than 40 dB
  CMRR). Low edge 0.254 V: the tail source is starved as the common mode falls; the follower keeps
  slope >= 0.8 well below the 40 dB ICMR low edge (0.717 V) because its criterion tolerates a
  strongly degraded input stage -- the two low edges are different criteria, not a contradiction.
- At fixed common mode the output stage spans 1.594 Vpp (98 % of VDD) at A >= 4, 1.483 Vpp at 40 dB
  and 1.310 Vpp at 60 dB at SS / -40 C. DR-002's ~1.39 Vpp output-headroom estimate is bracketed by
  the 40 / 60 dB figures: the output stage is roughly where the hand estimate put it; the follower
  does not reach it.
- The same holds at every grid point on the high side and at 11 / 15 on the low side; at 125 C the
  low side reaches the rail in both benches (TT/FF/SS/SF) except FS / 125 C (buffer 0.084 V,
  input-stage limited).
- Consequence for the evidence chain (no decision taken here): an output-stage resize alone would not
  move the unity-buffer edges at this sizing; the binding quantities are the input pair's drain
  headroom (high side) and the tail source's headroom (low side). No canonical sizing, topology,
  ratified target or existing `dc_swing` result is changed by this record.
