## Context

SKY130 drawn dimensions must be multiples of the 0.005 µm manufacturing grid.
DR-007's interpolated widths 4.634 µm and 7.819 µm are not (926.8 and
1563.8 grid steps). Generated off-grid devices fail `*.ongrid.1` DRC while
extraction preserves the illegal width and LVS is width-exact, so the
canonical schematic had to move onto the grid before the PMOS mirror could be
laid out. DR-007's own widths were quoted to ±0.001 µm of the gm/ID
interpolation, i.e. well inside the interpolation's resolution, so the
nearest legal widths are the natural repair.

## Decision

| Group | Devices | DR-007 W (µm) | **New W (µm)** | grid steps | `nf` | per-finger W (µm) | `m` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PMOS mirror/output, `L = 0.3` | `XM3`, `XM4`, `XM6` | 4.634 | **4.635** | 927 | 1 | 4.635 | 1, 1, 10 |
| NMOS bias/tail/sink, `L = 1.2` | `XMB1`, `XM5`, `XM7` | 7.819 | **7.820** | 1564 | 1 | 7.820 | 1, 2, 10 |

- Nearest legal width in each case (+0.001 µm, +0.022 % and +0.013 %); this
  is the smallest possible perturbation of the committed design point.
- **Per-finger/per-split legality**: every canonical MOS card has `nf = 1`,
  so the finger width equals `W` and is legal. Multiplicity is carried by
  `m` (whole-device copies), so there is no fractional `W/nf` and no split
  that could reintroduce a sub-grid dimension. If a layout later splits a
  device into fingers, `W/nf` must itself be a multiple of 0.005 µm
  (e.g. 4.635 = 3 × 1.545, 7.820 = 4 × 1.955 are legal; 2 × 2.3175 is not);
  `design/bin/grid_check.py` enforces this for `nf > 1` netlist cards.
- **Group ratios are preserved exactly** because each group moves as one
  width: `XM3 : XM4 : XM6 = 1 : 1 : 10` and `XMB1 : XM5 : XM7 = 1 : 2 : 10`
  (counts via `m`), so the zero-systematic-offset-by-construction property of
  DR-002/DR-007 still holds. `XM1`/`XM2` (6.030 µm) and `XRz`/`XCc` were
  already on the grid.
- `design/opamp_core.sch` and `design/netlist/opamp_core.spice` carry the same
  widths (netlist regenerated by headless xschem; `netlist_check.py` and the
  pair record agree).

Across all 15 points the largest per-point change is 0.0018 dB gain,
200 Hz GBW, 0.003 deg phase margin, 0.003 V/us fall SR and 1.6e-6 relative
power. **No binding-corner regression.** Ratified targets are unchanged:
GBW and rise SR remain met; fall SR (55 % of target) and swing (58.5 %)
remain the *open, unmet* rows they were under DR-007 — this record neither
closes nor worsens them.

One non-binding anomaly, reported rather than hidden: the output-swing
`vout_min` extraction at the FS corner moved by -0.021 V at -40 C and
+0.026 V at 27 C (swing -0.0257 Vpp, -1.9 % at FS/27 C; the FS/125 C point is
unchanged to 1e-6). The opposite signs at neighbouring temperatures on a
0.02 % width change indicate the swing metric's threshold crossing sitting on
a flat/nonconvergent portion of the DC sweep (the fleet flags nonconvergence
warnings there), not a sizing trend. These points are not binding (FS swing
is 1.23-1.62 Vpp against the SS/-40 C binding 0.9485 Vpp) and were not
massaged.

The secondary benches are likewise unchanged where comparable to the
`20261009-103006-566b9a5` record: ICMR worst width 0.4519 V @ SS/-40 C (no
corner covers the target window, as before), CMRR @100 Hz worst 55.11 dB (mid)
and 69.34 dB (window), step overshoot worst 4.87 % @ SF/27 C, 1 % settling
worst 40.5 ns @ SS/125 C.

Fleet note (not a failure): the runner image reports klt 0.5.0 against the
0.7.0 client (`runner_compatibility: mismatch` in the job metadata), as for
earlier fleet records; all units returned `pass`.

## Alternatives considered

- **Round down (4.630 / 7.815)**: equally legal, but -0.09 %/-0.05 % is a
  larger perturbation than rounding to nearest; no electrical reason to
  prefer it.
- **Re-interpolate on the gm/ID sweep**: the sweep resolution is coarser than
  0.001 µm; nearest-grid rounding is within the interpolation's own error.
- **Fingered devices** (`nf > 1`): would put the grid constraint on `W/nf`
  and complicate LVS width matching; not needed for the electrical decision
  and left to the layout issue.

## Spec lines affected

None. `spec/target-spec.md` targets and statuses are unchanged; its DR-007
citations remain correct to the quoted precision (the new records reproduce
every binding value).

## Consequences

- LVS against the layout is width-exact; the layout issue must draw
  4.635 µm and 7.820 µm (or legal fingerings of them).
- `design/bin/grid_check.py` now checks MOS widths by default (CI step
  renamed accordingly), so reintroducing 4.634/7.819 or any off-grid canonical
  MOS width fails `check_ci`. Tests in `tests/test_grid_check.py`.
- The item-8 characterization envelope in the block manifest still cites the
  older ICMR/CMRR record; refreshing it onto the new records is left to a
  follow-up (the new records are `dut_identity`-verified for the current
  design).
